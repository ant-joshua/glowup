# 1. Product Overview

The Creator Economy Platform enables users to become creators, share transformation journeys, publish routines, build audiences, and monetize their expertise through affiliate commerce, subscriptions, digital products, and future brand partnerships.

The objective is to transform GlowUp OS from a recommendation application into a creator-driven ecosystem.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Enable anyone to become a trusted transformation creator.

Creators should be able to:

- Share knowledge
- Build followers
- Monetize expertise
- Generate content with AI
- Earn recurring income
