# 1. Product Overview

The Affiliate Commerce Platform transforms GlowUp OS from a content platform into a commerce platform.

The objective is to enable:

- Users to discover products
- Creators to monetize recommendations
- Brands to increase conversions
- GlowUp OS to generate revenue

without owning inventory.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Every recommendation should be actionable.

Every creator should be monetizable.

Every product recommendation should have measurable ROI.
